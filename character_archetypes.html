Character Personality Archetypes

2125 characters clustered into 8 archetypes using K-means on 500 personality traits, then arranged by UMAP

How to read this visualization

How it was made

K-means clustering grouped all 2,125 characters into 8 archetypes based on their scores across 500 personality traits. Then UMAP — an algorithm that preserves neighborhood structure — reduced those 500 dimensions to 2D so we can draw it on a flat screen. Characters with similar personalities end up near each other.

What you're looking at

Each dot is one character, colored by their archetype. The 8 archetypes are named using the personality traits most extreme in that cluster — for example "villainous · traitorous" means that cluster's centroid scored highest on those two dimensions. The sidebar on the right shows portrait thumbnails for each archetype when you click it.

How to interpret it

Click any archetype in the sidebar list to highlight it and see its members. Click a dot on the map to jump to that character's archetype. Dots that sit between two color clusters are "borderline" personalities who share traits with both groups. Tight, dense clusters = very similar personalities; spread-out clusters = more varied members.
